  11. Drinking Water Operator Certification Exam Study Help (Utah Department of Environmental Quality, Division of Drinking Water) — https://deq.utah.gov/ddw/study-help
  12. Minnesota Rules 9400.1000, Examinations (Minnesota Office of the Revisor of Statutes) — https://www.revisor.mn.gov/rules/9400.1000/
