Frequently Asked Questions

What topics does Clearwell cover?

Clearwell covers the Water Treatment Grade 1 exam with 172 questions in 12 topics: treatment math, disinfection, filtration, drinking water standards, coagulation and settling, disinfection byproducts, microbial monitoring, compliance and reporting, safety, cross-connections, source water, and lead, copper and corrosion. Your state sets its own exam, so check its own study material too.

How many practice questions are included?

Clearwell 1.0 includes 172 original practice questions for Water Treatment Grade 1, each with an explanation and its public source.

Can I access Clearwell offline?

Yes. Every quiz works offline, even in airplane mode: study on the job site, during your commute, or anywhere without a connection. Downloading the app and buying or restoring the unlock need a connection, because they go through the App Store or Google Play. Your quiz data never leaves your device; Clearwell has no servers.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Clearwell doesn't require an account, login, email, or any personal information. Your progress is saved locally on your device. No account means no data collection and better privacy.

What happens to my progress if I reinstall the app?

Your quiz history and streak are stored only on your device, so if you delete and reinstall Clearwell, you start with a fresh score history. Your unlock isn't lost: on the paywall, tap Restore Purchase while signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account.

Is Clearwell a study guarantee or a study tool?

Clearwell is a study tool to help you prepare. Passing your state certification exam depends on your study effort, background experience, and familiarity with the material. Clearwell provides source-cited practice questions—but exam success ultimately depends on you.

Do you update questions to match current exam content?

Each question cites the source it was checked against. Corrections come in app updates, at no extra charge. Your state sets the exam itself, so check its own study material too.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. On iPhone, request one through your App Store account under Apple's refund policy (how to request a refund on the App Store). On Android, request one through Google Play (how to request a refund on Google Play).

Is Clearwell on Android?

Yes. The Android app has the same 172 questions, the same four modes and the same one-time unlock, bought through Google Play. It isn't on Google Play yet.

How do I know the questions are accurate?

Every question in Clearwell cites its public source: federal regulations (40 CFR and 29 CFR), EPA and USGS publications, and state agency material (Florida DEP, Utah DEQ, Minnesota Rules). The full list, with addresses, is on the home page, and the app names each question's source beside its answer, with a link. Every question was checked against its source before release, and you can check any answer yourself.

What if I think a question or answer is wrong?

If you find an error, please email us at support@8086.llc with details. Include the question's first words, your concern, and the source you're referencing. We take accuracy seriously and will investigate every report.

I have a feature request or feedback.

We'd love to hear from you! Email support@8086.llc with your suggestions. User feedback helps us improve Clearwell.
