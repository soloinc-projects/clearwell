The one purchase

Clearwell is free to download, with the first 3 questions of every mode. The one thing sold is the unlock: a one-time in-app purchase that opens all 172 questions ($12.99 in the U.S.; the store shows your local price). There is no subscription. Apple or Google sells it and handles any refund: see Apple's refund page or Google Play's refund policies.

What Clearwell is, and is not

Clearwell is a study aid for the Water Treatment Grade 1 exam. It is not a government app, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government agency, exam board or licensing agency. Your state sets its own exam, time limit and pass mark, so check its own study material too. Practicing with Clearwell doesn't guarantee a result on your exam.

Each question cites the public source its answer rests on, and the app links it, so you can check any answer yourself. If you think an answer is wrong, email support@8086.llc.
